About Haula Enterprises

Smart Life, Real Value — A leading Tanzanian enterprise delivering high-capacity haulage, express port clearing, security firm training, security systems, Cyber Security, enterprise software, and Haula Technology Hub.

Driven by our corporate slogan 'Smart Life, Real Value', Haula Enterprises provides integrated solutions across heavy freight haulage, commercial trading, security firm training, security systems & Cyber Security, enterprise software platforms (Dawafy OS), and ICT capacity building (Haula Technology Hub).

Driven by reliability, speed, and cutting-edge technology, we empower businesses across Tanzania and the SADC region with high-capacity haulage, seamless port clearing, specialized security training, security software, Cyber Security, and high-performance enterprise platforms like Dawafy Pharmacy OS.
